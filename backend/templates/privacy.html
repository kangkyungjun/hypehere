MarketLens — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: email address and nickname.
  • User content: posts, comments, likes, watchlist, and investment profile you create.
  • Device data: push notification token, device platform, and app language.
  • Moderation data: reports you submit and users you block, used to keep the community safe.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to operate the Service, deliver notifications you opt into, personalize stock insights, process subscriptions, and moderate user-generated content.

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party providers that may process limited data on our behalf, including RevenueCat (subscription management), Google AdMob (advertising), and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications). Their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You can request account deletion in the app. Upon a deletion request, your account and associated personal data are removed after a short grace period, except where retention is required by law.

5. Contact

Privacy questions: marketlens.service@gmail.com
